# Dataset and license survey

Date: 2026-11-08
Status: research notes; verify every license against the original source before
training or redistribution.

## 1. Conclusion

No dataset with a clean redistribution license and **online CJK handwriting
strokes** was found on Hugging Face or ModelScope. The usable online CJK data
remains the Tegaki/Tomoe corpora and the Arphic-licensed synthetic stroke data.

Most "handwriting" datasets on the model hubs are offline image datasets, and
several license tags are wrong or incomplete. The important distinction is:

- **online**: `(x, y)` stroke trajectories; suitable for Zinnia and online
  neural models;
- **offline**: scanned images; suitable for image OCR or derendering research
  (option C), not directly for Zinnia.

## 4. License traps seen in the survey

- `Teklia/CASIA-HWDB2-line` is tagged MIT, but its card describes CASIA data.
  The CASIA terms say academic use only; commercial use requires a separate
  license.
- `DatatangBeijing/...` is tagged Apache-2.0, but its card says the copyright
  belongs to Datatang and the dataset is commercial.
- `AlienKevin/kanjivg_klee` is tagged CC0, while KanjiVG is CC BY-SA 3.0.
- `Kratos-AI/KAI_handwriting-ocr` is tagged CC BY 4.0 but its card says
  non-commercial use.
- Several CASIA mirrors have no license at all.

Treat a hub license tag as metadata, not as legal permission.

## 5. Usable upstream data (not model hubs)

| Corpus | Input | License | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tomoe / Tegaki XML | Online strokes, zh_CN/zh_TW/ja | LGPL-2.1-or-later | Zinnia and online neural training; committed in `data/tomoe/` |
| Make Me a Hanzi `graphics.txt` | Synthetic stroke medians, ~9.5k characters | Arphic Public License | Character coverage; commercial use with attribution |
| AnimCJK | Japanese/Traditional glyph stroke geometry | Arphic Public License | Japanese/Traditional coverage and stroke order |
| KanjiVG | Japanese stroke order vectors | CC BY-SA 3.0 | Share-alike; isolate from GPL-only models |
| User-collected strokes | Online | Explicit consent | Best long-term source for writer style |

## 6. Practical recommendation

- Use **Tomoe + Make Me a Hanzi + AnimCJK** as the clean baseline.
- Keep **KanjiVG** in a separate license group/model if it is used at all.
- Do **not** train redistributable models on CASIA, ETL, ICDAR mirrors or
  Datatang samples without a commercial license.
- Use offline Apache/MIT datasets only for offline benchmarks or derendering
  experiments; they do not solve the online CJK data gap.
- Prefer self-collected online strokes over scraping ambiguous mirrors.
